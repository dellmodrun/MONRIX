What is MONRIX?
In one line
A desktop app that trades on Solana and keeps the profits in Monero or shielded Zcash, with a VPN built in. Your machine, your keys, no account.
Why
Every Solana trade is public forever. Monero and shielded Zcash are not. Most people need five websites and an exchange account to get from one to the other. This is one window, and it tells you who can see what at each step.
New to this?
If "seed", "shielded" or "swap service" mean nothing to you, read the Beginner's guide first. Ten minutes, no jargon, and every page on this site has a plain-language post at the top before the technical replies.

How a trade becomes private money, in three steps
  1. Trade on Solana in the Terminal. Public, like all of Solana. Fund that key only with what you mean to trade.
  2. Route the profit through the Exchange board: pick a swap service with the rate, the speed and the ID requirement in front of you.
  3. Settle into your own Monero wallet, or shielded Zcash, running on your machine. From here on, nobody is watching the balance.

No server, no account, no analytics, no phone-home. What is and is not covered is written down in full on the Security board.

So the tunnel ships inside the wallet
  • WireGuard, built in. Runs inside MONRIX itself. No driver, no administrator rights, no second app. Paste a config from Mullvad, Proton, IVPN, AirVPN or your own server and connect. Every request the app makes leaves through the tunnel, DNS included.
  • Fails closed. If the handshake is lost, requests stop. There is no path back to the clear network for the app's own traffic.
  • Or Tor. Point the same switch at a Tor SOCKS port and the identical set of requests goes through Tor.
  • Proof, not a badge. Exit-IP check through the tunnel itself, Tor-exit detection, a DNS-leak test that resolves real names, and the WebRTC check above.
  • Honest scope. The built-in tunnel covers what MONRIX sends. It cannot capture other programs or a browser extension such as Phantom. For whole-machine coverage it also drives a system WireGuard tunnel, and the interface says which layer protects what.
