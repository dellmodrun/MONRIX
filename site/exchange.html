Exchange: from SOL to XMR, with the disclosure in front of the button« on: today »

This is how your Solana profit becomes Monero or Zcash. You send SOL to a swap service, and a few minutes later XMR or ZEC arrives in your Vault. Think of it as a currency exchange booth on the internet. For those few minutes you are trusting the booth, so the app shows you everything about it before you pay.

How to use it

  1. Choose the direction, for example SOL → XMR, and the amount.
  2. The app asks several booths at once and lists them: how much you get, how long it takes, and an ID rating from A to D. A never asks for your passport. D often does, sometimes after you have already paid. Prefer A and B.
  3. Pick one. A red box appears saying exactly what this booth will learn about you: the amount, your Monero address, and your IP unless the VPN is on.
  4. Click order. The app shows you the address to pay and the exact amount. You pay it from the trading screen. The order is tracked until the Monero arrives.
Three habits
Turn the VPN on first. Let the app give the booth a fresh Monero address (it does this by default). Keep amounts boring; round numbers at odd hours stand out.

Routes

  • XMR ⇄ SOL, ZEC ⇄ SOL, USDC (Solana) ⇄ XMR or ZEC. SOL ⇄ USDC on-chain belongs in the Terminal.
  • Quotes fan out to all enabled providers in parallel with a 20-second budget each, sorted by output; failures show the provider's own error.
  • Each quote shows rate, amount out, minimum and maximum, ETA, KYC rating, fixed or floating, latency, and notes such as log policy or geographic restrictions.

Orders, failures, refunds

  • A refund address is requested on every order and prefilled from your own wallets. A missing refund address is flagged on the order.
  • Orders are stored locally with the provider reference and polled. Transitions to review, refunding, refunded, failed or expired are logged at warning level.
  • Nothing is sent automatically. The application shows the deposit address and exact amount; you pay from the Vault or your Solana wallet.

What the provider learns

The disclosure is generated per order: your IP path (Tor exit, VPN exit, or real), the deposit transaction, the settle address, amounts and timing. Monero settlement proposes a fresh subaddress. Zcash legs are transparent on every provider we know of, so the application tells you to shield after settlement. Solana legs tie your wallet to the provider on-chain.

Quote
These are instant exchangers, not mixers. Use the tunnel, use fresh addresses, keep amounts unremarkable, and prefer providers with a no-log policy and a favourable KYC rating.
